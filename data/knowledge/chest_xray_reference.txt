Chest X-ray research reference

Chest radiography is commonly used as an imaging examination of the chest.

Radiology interpretation may describe findings such as lung opacity,
consolidation, pleural effusion, pneumothorax, or other abnormalities.

A chest X-ray classification model may assist with research-oriented
image classification, but its output should not be treated as a definitive
clinical diagnosis.

Pneumonia can have imaging findings involving lung opacity or consolidation,
but imaging findings must be interpreted together with clinical history,
symptoms, examination, and other relevant investigations.

An AI model may produce false positive and false negative predictions.
